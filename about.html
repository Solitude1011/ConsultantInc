Who we are

Introduction

Laynes Shipping Services Limited. A full service Logistics Provided, located at 1st Floor, Victoria Suites, #14-17 Park Street, Port of Spain, Trinidad, has developed a reputation as a customer- centric, vibrant service organization that is success-driven

We encourage continuous feedback from our stakeholders to improve the services we offer, proactively manage changes that impact on our business and exercise initiative to capitalize on opportunities.

Our commitment

Vision statement

Recognizing our responsibility in serving the customers we represent, and the competitiveness in the current market place, Layne's Shipping services Limited, is committed to providing cost effective solutions to our clients logistics needs.

One of our major objectives, is to operate within the legal framework of Trinidad and Tobago and always ensure our name and the name of our principals always remain associated with Honesty and Integrity.

Our company

Company Overview

Layne's Shipping Services Limited was formed recognizing the need to provide a full service offering to its principals. The aggregate of experience within Layne's, when tallied spans over 60 years in the industry.

We partner with the more reputable service providers on the island and ensure strictest adherence to the prevailing statutory requirements and HSSE Codes
