Health, Safety, Security and Environment (HSSE)

Layne's Shipping Services Ltd is committed to ensuring the safety, health and welfare at work of all its employees and intends to provide the following:-

  • Safe equipment for the conduct of its business.
  • A safe and healthy place of work including adequate welfare amenities.
  • Suitable protective equipment where necessary for the safety and good health of its employees.
  • Safe systems.
  • Information, instruction, training and supervision are a necessity for safety and health at work.
