Our services

What we do

Our many services include:

01

Customs brokerage

The professional service of managing international trade regulations, preparing border paperwork, calculating duties and taxes, and securing official clearance for imported or exported goods.

02

Customs Consultancy Services

Expert guidance on import and export regulations, tariff classifications, and compliance to help businesses move goods across international borders smoothly.

03

Logistics/Project Management/W-housing

Coordination of logistics activities, project requirements, and warehousing to help keep goods, schedules, and resources organized.

04

Shipping Agency Services

Port-call support and coordination for vessels, including communication with local service providers and assistance with shipping documentation.

05

Work permit/Visa arrangements

Assistance with coordinating work permit and visa applications and related documentation for personnel, subject to the requirements of the relevant authorities.

06

Transportation (Normal and Abnormal Loads)

Transportation coordination for standard cargo and oversized or abnormal loads, with planning suited to the shipment's requirements.

About us

Introduction

Laynes Shipping Services Limited. A full service Logistics Provided, located at 1st Floor, Victoria Suites, #14-17 Park Street, Port of Spain, Trinidad, has developed a reputation as a customer- centric, vibrant service organization that is success-driven

We encourage continuous feedback from our stakeholders to improve the services we offer, proactively manage changes that impact on our business and exercise initiative to capitalize on opportunities.

Our commitment

Vision statement

Recognizing our responsibility in serving the customers we represent, and the competitiveness in the current market place, Layne's Shipping services Limited, is committed to providing cost effective solutions to our clients logistics needs.

One of our major objectives, is to operate within the legal framework of Trinidad and Tobago and always ensure our name and the name of our principals always remain associated with Honesty and Integrity.

Who we are

Company Overview

Layne's Shipping Services Limited was formed recognizing the need to provide a full service offering to its principals. The aggregate of experience within Layne's, when tallied spans over 60 years in the industry.
